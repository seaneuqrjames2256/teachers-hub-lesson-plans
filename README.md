## Overview

The Teacher's Hub provides educators with one organized web location for the resources they rely on throughout the school day. Curriculum references, lesson plans, assessment materials, and policy documents can be accessed together instead of being scattered among multiple files and locations.

The platform is suited to schools and education groups seeking a more structured approach to planning and coordination. Keeping essential materials in a shared space makes resources easier to locate, supports teamwork, and helps promote consistency in learning experiences.

## Using the Platform

The Teacher's Hub can serve as a shared workspace for teaching preparation and coordination:

1. Launch the platform in a modern browser.
2. Find the curriculum materials related to the subject or term being prepared.
3. Use the lesson plans and assessment resources to shape classroom activities.
4. Consult school policies when teaching plans need to reflect local requirements.
5. Work from the same resource set with colleagues to maintain consistent planning.

A typical preparation sequence may look like this:

- Choose the appropriate curriculum area
- Examine the available lesson materials
- Develop assessment activities and teaching notes
- Compare shared resources with team-wide expectations
